## 1. Background

1.1 The Synthetic Digital Services Branch (fictional) requires a web portal, the Regional Services Portal, through which members of the public can find regional service information and book appointments.

1.2 This statement of work is fictional and was written for a prototype demonstration. It does not describe a real procurement.

## 5. Standards

5.1 Referenced standard. The referenced standard for this contract is the Revised Section 508 Standards (36 CFR Part 1194), which incorporate WCAG 2.0 Level AA by reference, success criterion 2.1.1 Keyboard.

5.2 Keyboard operability (D-03). All functionality of the delivered release, including the appointment booking workflow, shall be operable through a keyboard interface without requiring specific timings for individual keystrokes. Focus shall not become trapped in any component.

5.3 Build coverage. The accessibility evaluation relied on for acceptance shall cover the exact build delivered under D-01, identified by its build identifier. Evaluation of an earlier build does not satisfy this requirement.
