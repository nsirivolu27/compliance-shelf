## 1. Purpose

1.1 This brief opens a fictional evidence review of contract file SYN-VA-PORTAL-2026-001 for the Regional Services Portal. It sets the objective, scope, business question, roles and ground rules for the review.

1.2 This pack is synthetic. The procurement, offices, vendor, builds, defects and records are fictional. No real contract, claimant, beneficiary or employee information is used. People are identified only by synthetic role IDs.

1.3 This brief was prepared for an independent Build Challenge prototype. It was not created, approved, purchased or endorsed by Accenture, the Department of Veterans Affairs, or any agency.

## 2. Objective and business question

2.1 Objective: give the reviewer an organized, cited view of the evidence in the contract file so that the reviewer can judge whether delivery and accessibility expectations are supported, and can prepare an accountable audit handoff.

2.2 Business question:

Does the supplied contract file contain adequate, current, traceable evidence for a reviewer to assess the stated delivery and accessibility expectations?

2.3 "Adequate" means the evidence addresses the stated criterion. "Current" means it applies to the build that was actually delivered and reflects the latest test results. "Traceable" means each claim can be followed to a specific document ID, version and passage.

## 3. Scope

3.1 In scope: the documents listed in section 7, as received by the review team, including later versions and remediation submissions as they are logged in the deliverable register (SYN-VAP-DOC-02).

3.2 Out of scope: legal sufficiency of the contract, payment decisions, benefits decisions, independent re-testing of the portal, and any records disposition decision.

| Area | In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|---|
| Accessibility evidence | Whether the file's evidence covers the delivered build and the SOW criterion | Re-testing the portal |
| Delivery traceability | Whether transmitted artifacts match registered deliverables | Code or configuration review |
| Acceptance review | Whether SOW §6 and §7.3 conditions are documented | Making the acceptance decision |
| Records | Whether hold status is recorded and respected | Applying a records schedule or authorizing disposition |

## 5. Oversight background

5.1 The VA Office of Inspector General published report 24-02142-105 on July 1, 2025, reviewing accessibility in VA procurement of information and communication technology. It is cited here only as background explaining why validated accessibility evidence for the delivered product matters to a reviewer.

5.2 The report is oversight background, not a contract requirement. No recommendation in it is treated as binding in this review. The applicable standards for this fictional contract are those cited in SOW SYN-VAP-DOC-01 §5.

## 6. What the platform does and does not do

| The platform does | The platform does not |
|---|---|
| Organize the supplied documents by document ID and version | Certify legal or regulatory compliance |
| Show cited passages next to the check they relate to | Authorize payment or acceptance |
| Record reviewer judgments with reasons and the evidence cited | Decide benefits or eligibility |
| Keep earlier document versions alongside later ones | Authorize records destruction or release a hold |
| Prepare an audit handoff the reviewer can sign off | Discover exceptions on its own; findings are the reviewer's |
